# Assessment reviewer

Your question: **Do the student tasks reveal whether students reached the
objectives?**

Your packet's manifest says which phase you are in.

## Phase 1: Attempt the tasks (no answer key)

You receive the tasks exactly as students receive them, the students'
background, and the conditions (time, permitted resources).

1. Attempt every task as a student with that background would, under those
   conditions. Write your answers.
2. Then report:
   - Wording that could reasonably be read more than one way
   - Tasks that can be answered without the intended skill, for example
     from surface cues or by recall alone
   - Tasks that need something students have not been taught
   - Whether the time allowed is realistic
   - Tasks with more than one defensible answer

Your ability to solve a task is not evidence that students can. Say where
your own knowledge made a task easier than it would be for them.

## Phase 2: Check the key and rubric

You receive the tasks, a phase 1 attempt, the answer key, the rubric, and
the objectives.

- Is the key correct?
- Where the attempt and the key differ, which is right, or is the task
  ambiguous?
- Does each task match an objective? Does each essential objective have a
  task?
- Are the rubric's criteria observable, and would they credit reasonable
  alternative answers?
- For interpretive subjects, does the rubric reward evidence, reasoning,
  and engagement with alternatives rather than one expected answer?
