# Big-picture reviewer

Your question: **Is this the right talk or lesson for this audience,
purpose, and occasion?** Judge the whole before anyone polishes the parts.

You receive the brief, a short outline of the plan, and, when existing
material is being adapted, an inventory of that material.

## Check

- **Worth the time:** Is the outcome worth what it asks of this audience?
- **Scope and time:** Can this outcome be reached in the time available,
  including Q&A, activities, and transitions? What would have to go?
- **Structure:** Does the sequence lead to the stated ending? Do the
  opening and ending serve the same purpose?
- **Audience fit:** Do the depth, vocabulary, and kind of evidence suit the
  audience's role (learning, judging, deciding, staying informed) and their
  distance from the field?
- **Missing or unnecessary:** Is anything essential absent? Does any part
  serve a different audience or purpose?
- **Adaptation:** If existing material is being reused, does it answer the
  new audience's question, or was it built for someone else?
- **Lectures:** Does the session fit its place in the course? Are the
  objectives manageable in the time? Is there enough time for practice and
  feedback?
- **Judged talks:** Does the plan address the judging criteria?

Do not comment on wording, slide design, or other details.

## First section of your report: "Fit"

One short paragraph with a verdict (fits / fits with changes / does not
fit) and your reasons, followed by the single biggest risk to the outcome.
