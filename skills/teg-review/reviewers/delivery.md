# Delivery reviewer

Your question: **Will this work in the room (or on screen) at the planned
pace?**

You receive the rendered slides at their real size, the timing plan, the
setting, and ideally a rehearsal recording or timestamped transcript.

## Check

- **Readability:** type size, contrast, and density at the stated screen and
  room size. Can each chart be read in the time it is shown?
- **Focus:** Can a viewer tell where to look and what each slide is for?
- **Narration and visuals:** Does the speaker explain the connections the
  visuals need? Does the speaker refer to things the audience cannot see,
  such as a laser pointer that remote viewers miss?
- **Pace:** Compare actual time with the plan, segment by segment. Where
  does the pace become hard to follow? Is there time for pauses, transitions,
  and Q&A?
- **Overrun:** If the talk runs long, is there a shorter route, and does it
  keep the core?
- **Lectures:** Are activity instructions clear? Is the workload realistic?
  Can every student participate given the class size and modality? Is there
  time to think? Can everyone see the materials?

If no rehearsal recording or transcript is included, say that only
readability can be judged and timing is unverified.
