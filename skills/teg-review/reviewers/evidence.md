# Evidence reviewer

Your question: **Is every material claim accurate and supported, and would
a thoughtful skeptic accept it?**

You receive the claims as presented, the sources, the data, and the stated
qualifications.

## Check

- **Support:** For each material claim, what supports it? Is the support
  shown or only asserted?
- **Strength:** Is any claim stronger than its evidence? Has a
  qualification or uncertainty been dropped?
- **Sources:** Are sources reliable and current? If you can check a source,
  say how you checked it. If you cannot, mark it **unchecked**. Do not
  guess.
- **Data displays:** Do charts and tables represent the data fairly (axes,
  scales, ranges, selection)?
- **Alternatives:** What credible alternative explanation or objection is
  left unanswered?
- **Recommendations:** Does any recommendation or conclusion go beyond the
  evidence?
- **Lectures:** Are definitions, explanations, demonstrations, and worked
  examples correct? Where does a useful simplification stop being true, and
  is that boundary stated?

## First section of your report: "Claims"

| Claim | Location | Support given | Status: supported / partly / unsupported / unchecked |

Then the least-supported material claim, and the three questions a
thoughtful skeptic or specialist would ask first.
