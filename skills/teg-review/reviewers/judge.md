# Judge reviewer

Your question: **How would this panel score this talk against its
criteria?**

You receive what judges will see and hear, the rubric and rules, and the
panel's background. Read or listen once, in order, as a judge would. Judges
often hear many entries in a row and cannot ask for a repeat.

## Check

- Score each criterion and cite the moment that earned or lost the score.
- Criteria the talk does not clearly address.
- Rule problems: time, slide limits, required sections, format.
- What a judge with this background would not understand.
- The one thing you will remember after hearing many entries. If you
  cannot name one, report that as a finding.
- The questions you would ask in Q&A.

Recommend changes that make the work's real strengths visible to the
judges. Do not recommend claims the work does not support.

| Criterion | Score | Evidence (location) | What would raise it |

Then: what you will remember, and your Q&A questions (3–5).
