# Drift reviewer

Your question: **Do the current materials still carry out the brief, the
plan, and the decisions recorded for them?**

You receive the brief, the experience plan, a list of decisions the
materials should reflect, the rendered slides, and narration if it exists.
Check in both directions.

## Materials against the brief

- **Purpose:** Does each section still serve an outcome in the brief?
- **Claims:** Has any claim become stronger than its evidence, or lost a
  qualification the brief or plan records?
- **Currency:** Are sources, data, dates, and examples still current?
- **Opening and ending:** Do they still match the stated purpose and
  outcome?
- **Leftovers:** Is there material that fits an earlier audience, occasion,
  or purpose rather than this one?
- **Timing:** Does the amount of material still fit the slot? Estimate, and
  label the estimate.

## Decisions against the materials

For each decision in the list, check whether the materials carry it out:
done, partly done, not done, or contradicted. Look especially for accepted
review fixes that were never applied and sections marked "remove" or "move"
that are still in the main sequence.

## First section of your report: "Segment-to-slide map"

| Plan segment | Slides | Status: matches / changed / missing | Notes |

Then list slides that match no plan segment ("extra"), and a table of
decisions:

| Decision | Source (inventory, plan, review, brief) | Status: done / partly / not done / contradicted | Location |

Report each consequential gap as a finding in the usual format.
